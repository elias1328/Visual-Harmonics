Visual Harmonics

Which notes sound good together depends on the sound playing them. Every real sound is a stack of overtones, and when the overtones of two notes rub against each other, we hear roughness.

Here harmony is calculated from the sound itself: we measure each instrument's overtones and compute, for every pitch, how smoothly it blends. Nothing is looked up in a music-theory table, so a bell, a piano and a sine wave each get their own map of what fits.

Visual Harmonics is free & open source. Play, break things, and look inside.
